Terms and rules

Last updated 6 November 2026

By using FCMS Library you agree to these rules.

Using the library

  • The library is free and is provided as it is, without any guarantee that a file is correct, complete or current.
  • Follow your school's rules on academic honesty when you use any material.
  • Do not try to overload, scrape at scale or break into the site.

Contributing a file

  • Only upload material you made, or material you are allowed to share.
  • Allowed types are PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text and common images. The maximum size is 6 MB. Files over 3 MB must be compressed before they can be approved.
  • Do not upload malware, private information about other people, or anything illegal or abusive.
  • Every upload is reviewed. Administrators may reject or remove any file at any time, with or without a reason.
  • The name you enter is shown as the contributor name and is not verified. Do not use another person's name.

Copyright and removal

If you own material shown here and want it removed, use the takedown page.

Administrators

Administrators review uploads and manage the library. They sign in with their own accounts. Their decisions about what is published are final.

Changes

These rules may change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
