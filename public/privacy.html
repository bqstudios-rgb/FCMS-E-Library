Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

FCMS Library is a free library of course materials. Students browse and download without an account. This page explains what the site stores and why.

What we collect

  • When you contribute a file: the name you type (we do not verify it), the file, its title, course and type. The site gives you a ticket number so you can check the status of your upload.
  • When you send feedback: your message, and your name only if you choose to give it.
  • When you suggest a course: the course name, code and level you enter.
  • Downloads: we count how many times each file is downloaded per day. We do not record who downloaded it.
  • IP address: used briefly to limit repeated uploads, downloads, searches and sign-in attempts. It is used to protect the site and not to profile anyone.

Cookies and tracking

  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Students do not need a cookie to use the site.
  • Cloudflare's security check, which appears on some forms, may use a short-lived technical cookie to tell people from bots.
  • Administrators receive one sign-in cookie so the site knows they are signed in.

Who can see what

  • Everyone can see the title, course, contributor name, file size and type of approved materials.
  • Pending uploads are visible only to administrators until they are approved or rejected.
  • Feedback and suggestions are visible only to administrators.

Where data is kept

The site runs on Cloudflare, which hosts the pages and the database. Uploaded files are stored with Dbrij, an S3-compatible storage service. These providers process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Approved materials stay until an administrator removes them.
  • Rejected uploads and their files are deleted about 30 days after rejection.
  • If a file was compressed before approval, the original larger copy is deleted during routine cleanup.
  • Feedback is kept until an administrator deletes it.

Your choices

  • Use a nickname or initials when you contribute if you prefer not to show your name.
  • To have a file removed, follow the steps on the takedown page.
  • For any privacy question, use the feedback form on the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
